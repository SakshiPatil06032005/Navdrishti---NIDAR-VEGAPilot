============================================================
10. BATTERY INTEGRATION
============================================================

Actual battery voltage ke liye:

- Battery-monitoring IC/module, ya
- Properly calculated resistor divider + ADC

Use karna hoga.

Battery ko directly Arduino pin par connect nahi karna chahiye jab tak:

- Maximum battery voltage verify na ho.
- Arduino ADC limit verify na ho.
- Divider ratio calculate na ho.
- Resistor power rating check na ho.
- Grounding verify na ho.
- Overvoltage/reverse-polarity protection na ho.

Battery percentage ke liye:

- Battery chemistry.
- Cell count.
- Full/empty voltage.
- Capacity.
- Charge/discharge curve.
- Calibration.

Required honge. Simple voltage-to-percent mapping rough estimate hi hogi.

Battery current/power ke liye:

- Current sensor ya shunt monitor.
- Correct current range.
- Correct polarity.
- Peak current handling.
- Heat and noise verification.

Required honge.

Arduino USB power ko mission battery status mat samajhna.

============================================================
11. CONNECTION AND SENSOR STATUS
============================================================

Controller status:

- CONNECTING: Connection try ho raha hai.
- CONNECTED: Recent valid packets aa rahe hain.
- RECONNECTING: Connection lost, retry chal rahi hai.
- DISCONNECTED: COM port open nahi ho raha.
- COMMUNICATION ERROR: Invalid packet/read error.
- STALE DATA: Time par valid packet nahi aaya.

Sensor status alag hona chahiye:

- VALID
- DEGRADED
- FAILED
- NO DATA

COM port open hona sirf itna batata hai ki transport open hua. Isse yeh prove
nahi hota ki GPS, IMU, barometer aur battery sensor sab properly work kar rahe
hain.
